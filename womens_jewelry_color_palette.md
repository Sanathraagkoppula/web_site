# Luxury Women's Jewelry Website --- Color Palette

**Style:** Luxury, feminine, elegant\
**Collection:** Women's gold and diamond jewelry\
**Palette:** Cream + Champagne Gold + Soft Rose

## 1. Core Colors

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Color                   Hex Code                Recommended Use
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Warm Cream              `#F8F5EF`               Main page background,
                                                  hero sections

  Champagne Gold          `#D1B078`               Primary buttons,
                                                  highlights, decorative
                                                  accents

  Soft Rose               `#C68F91`               Small accents,
                                                  collection labels,
                                                  subtle hover states

  Deep Brown              `#38302D`               Headings, body text,
                                                  navigation

  White                   `#FFFFFF`               Navbar, product cards,
                                                  form surfaces

                                                  secondary gold details

                                                  banners, dividers

                                                  promotional highlights
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Suggested Color Balance

-   **50%** cream and white
-   **25%** beige and other warm neutrals
-   **15%** deep brown for text and contrast
-   **5--10%** champagne gold and soft rose accents

Keep pink/rose accents limited so the website feels luxurious rather
than overly pink.

## 4. Typography Pairing

-   **Headings:** Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond
-   **Body text and UI:** Inter
-   Use elegant serif headings with clean sans-serif body text.
-   Use letter spacing sparingly for small uppercase labels.

## 6. Design Guidelines

1.  Use cream or white for most of the page.
2.  Use champagne gold for primary calls to action and subtle decorative
    details.
3.  Reserve soft rose for small accents rather than large backgrounds.
4.  Keep product photography on white or warm neutral backgrounds.
5.  Use deep brown text for a softer luxury feel than pure black.
6.  Avoid heavy shadows, excessive gradients, and too many competing
    accent colors.
7.  Check text contrast and keyboard focus states for accessibility.

## Recommended Final Palette

**Warm Cream `#F8F5EF` + Champagne Gold `#D1B078` + Soft Rose
`#C68F91` + Deep Brown `#38302D` + White `#FFFFFF`.**

This combination is suitable for a premium women's gold and diamond
jewelry store and works across desktop and mobile layouts.
