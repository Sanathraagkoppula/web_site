Download CSV for Shopify

In Shopify: Products → Import → choose the file → tick “Overwrite products with matching handles” → Upload and preview → Import. Edited products replace their old values; new ones are added.

Deleted products can't be removed by CSV — delete them in Shopify admin. They're listed here after export so you know which ones.

Update gold coin prices

Enter today's rate. Every product tagged gold-coins is re-priced from its weight option (e.g. “5 g”): weight × rate × (1 + premium %), rounded to the nearest rupee.
